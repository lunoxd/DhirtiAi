3. HOW IT WORKS (UNDER THE HOOD)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. DETERMINISTIC SCORING ENGINE:
   - 10 structured questions scored on a transparent 0–100 scale.
   - Evaluates risk levels: Stable (<25), Mild (25-49), Elevated (50-69), High (70-84), Critical (>=85).
   - Compares today's score against past check-ins to detect trends (IMPROVING, STABLE, INCREASING).

B. GROQ AI ANALYSIS & CHATBOT:
   - Groq AI (qwen/qwen3.6-27b) evaluates qualitative written notes.
   - Provides empathetic, trauma-informed, non-diagnostic guidance without clinical jargon.
   - Filtered by cleanAiResponse() to ensure clean, short, human replies.

C. SAFETY OVERRIDE & CLINICAL TRIAGE:
   - If a user reports feeling unsafe or severe distress (safetyConcern: true), the system:
     1. Immediately flags the check-in as HIGH/CRITICAL.
     2. Displays the 24/7 Crisis Support Modal (Tele-MANAS 14416).
     3. Transmits the case to the Doctor Triage Queue (/doctor) for clinician callback.
